# PrepPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-02

We collect only what we need to provide the Service.

## What we collect
- Account info: email and password (managed by Firebase Authentication), and optional display name.
- Profile/plan data you enter (weight, goals, preferences, saved plans).
- Optional diagnostics/telemetry if you opt in.
- Basic local settings cached on your device.

## How we use it
- Provide and improve the Service.
- Generate meal plans and shopping lists.
- Maintain account access and security.
- Diagnose issues if you opt in.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data. We share only with service providers needed to run the app (e.g., Firebase), and only diagnostics if you opt in.

## Your choices
You can update your profile, opt out of telemetry, and request account deletion at any time.
